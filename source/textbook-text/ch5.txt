  1  Unit 3
  2  Health and Well-being
  3  About the Unit
  4  This unit discusses the important role
  5  of balanced food, exercise and rest for
  6  a healthy life.
  7  Everybody needs to stay healthy
  8  and happy. This unit familiarises
  9  students with the different types of
 10  food which contribute to the body
 11  building,
 12  providing
 13  energy
 14  and
 15  protection from diseases. This unit
 16  also introduces the six major tastes
 17  Shada Rasa with examples, and
 18  guides students about balanced food
 19  and the need of adequate clean water.
 20  This unit also focuses on mindful
 21  eating, need for daily exercises, proper
 22  rest and gratitude for all resources.
 23  It shows the hard efforts of so many
 24  elements,
 25  people
 26  and
 27  animals
 28  associated with the production of each
 29  grain. It teaches students to care for
 30  each grain and never waste food. It
 31  also provides examples of exercises
 32  and games for proper health care,
 33  and indicates the necessity of rest
 34  for everybody. The unit also gives
 35  examples of different communities
 36  who care for their crops and respect
 37  the contribution of all concerned.

  1  Note to the Teacher
  2  This unit consists of Chapter 5 — Food for Health
  3  and Chapter 6 — Happy and Healthy Living. The
  4  key concepts covered in these chapters are listed
  5  below.
  6  Chapter 5
  7  Teacher to Facilitate
  8  •
  9  Through an engaging food festival in
 10  school, students will explore the diversity
 11  of food and develop an understanding
 12  about balanced food. Learners will be able
 13  to appreciate the need for including the
 14  energy rich, body-building and disease
 15  preventive food items in their diets.
 16  •
 17  This chapter also discusses a variety of
 18  traditional food items which are used in our
 19  country and its importance in maintaining
 20  good health of all members in every family.
 21  •
 22  Teacher will arrange
 23  age-appropriate charts,
 24  flashcards, and short videos
 25  on food groups, cooking
 26  methods, and balanced
 27  food. Students will be
 28  guided to record their
 29  observations.
 30  •
 31  Organise a ‘food mela’
 32  where students will bring
 33  home-cooked meals, display
 34  regional food thalis, and
 35  discuss food diversity.
 36  Facilitate hands-on tasks
 37  like food collages and
 38  mindful eating exercises.
 39  •
 40  Organise ‘mindful eating’
 41  activity for students to
 42  enable them to realise the
 43  value of food, avoid wastage
 44  of food, practice mindful
 45  eating, regular exercises
 46  and rest for body.
 47  •
 48  Encourage children to
 49  observe food labels, cooking
 50  methods and taste textures.
 51  Assign take-home activities,
 52  such as maintaining a
 53  food diary and designing a
 54  balanced meal plate.
 55  •
 56  Invite a nutritionist or a
 57  local farmer to discuss
 58  healthy eating and food
 59  sources. Also organise
 60  a visit to a community
 61  kitchen or a local farm to
 62  explore the food production
 63  and storage.
 64  Chapter 6
 65  •
 66  •
 67  This chapter discusses a need for health
 68  and well-being. It depicts the journey of
 69  a grain and shows how several elements
 70  of nature, people and other animals
 71  contribute to this journey. Students will be
 72  able to learn and respect each effort, and
 73  feel thankful to everyone’s contribution. It
 74  also reminds them not to waste any food.
 75  This chapter also guides students to
 76  do mindful eating, exercise every day,
 77  rest well, and practice gratitude to all
 78  resources. It also indicates the need for a
 79  safe environment for everyone.

  1  Say ‘No’ to Junk Food
  2  There are some foods that are not healthy if we
  3  consume them regularly and in large quantities. They
  4  contain high amounts of oil, salt, sugar, etc. These
  5  are known as ‘junk food’. A few examples of junk
  6  food are soft drinks, chips, bread pakoda, burgers,
  7  pizza, and over-processed, packaged food. Fresh
  8  home-cooked food is better than over-processed or
  9  packaged food.
