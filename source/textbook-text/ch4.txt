 19  Let us prepare a natural dye.
 20  Step 1. Collect some flowers, leaves, barks, and
 21  roots, such as beetroot, hibiscus, and
 22  marigold that have colouring properties.
 23  Step 2. Boil any of the collected plant parts in
 24  approximately 1 – 2 litres of water for 30
 25  minutes to an hour covering the container
 26  with a plate. (Please take help from your
 27  teacher or an elder.)
 28  Step 3. Strain the liquid to remove the plant
 29  materials, leaving behind the natural dye.
 30  Now, try to paint a piece of cloth such as a lightcoloured handkerchief or hand towel using the dye
 31  you have prepared. Note that you need to soak
 32  the cloth overnight, squeeze it and dry it the next
