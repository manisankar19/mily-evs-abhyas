  1  Unit 1
  2  Our Community
  3  About the Unit
  4  We all depend on each other in
  5  some way or another. We live in
  6  our communities, sharing space
  7  and resources. Regular interactions
  8  and celebrations enrich our bonds,
  9  creating a sense of belongingness.
 10  Communities thrive on cooperation,
 11  communication, and mutual respect in
 12  their social and natural surroundings.
 13  Students
 14  explore
 15  community
 16  institutes around them including
 17  schools, hospitals, banks and markets.
 18  They understand the role of people,
 19  tools used, and their contribution to
 20  the development of a more inclusive
 21  and sustainable society.
 22  They get familiarised with how
 23  transportation helps in the movement
 24  of people and various goods. Similarly,
 25  they also learn how the communication
 26  has improved steadily. With time, both
 27  transportation and communication
 28  have improved connectivity.
 29  They develop a sense of direction
 30  and ability to locate various places
 31  through landmarks by drawing,
 32  sketching, identifying and reading
 33  maps.

  1  Note to the Teacher
  2  This unit consists of Chapter 1— Living Together
  3  and Chapter 2— Exploring Our Neighbourhood.
  4  The key concepts covered in these chapters are
  5  listed below.
  6  Chapter 1
  7  •
  8  •
  9  Teacher to Facilitate
 10  ‘Living
 11  Together’
 12  helps
 13  students
 14  understand the community as a space of
 15  togetherness and interdependence. The
 16  story of Van Mahotsav helps students value
 17  their natural surroundings and public
 18  properties through mutual cooperation,
 19  and care.
 20  They also discover a culture of sharing and
 21  collaboration in all festivals celebrated by
 22  the different communities in the country.
 23  Students will learn to lead and work
 24  together, making a difference to their own
 25  life and the community.
 26  Chapter 2
 27  •
 28  Students look at various types of transports
 29  around them. They also learn about various
 30  modes of communication. They realise how
 31  transportation and communication have
 32  evolved over the years. Faster travels and
 33  communications have made significant
 34  changes to our lives, and activities in the
 35  world.
 36  •
 37  Students will get to know the role of
 38  various community institutes. They also
 39  learn the basics of financial literacy by
 40  interacting with people in banks. They
 41  will learn to draw and read simple maps
 42  through a series of activities. They will also
 43  learn to identify landmarks and directions
 44  to navigate their surroundings.
 45  •
 46  The teacher would
 47  arrange relevant, low-cost
 48  materials like maps, charts,
 49  photographs, observation
 50  sheets and other materials
 51  for activities.
 52  •
 53  Organise short field visits
 54  to places like post offices,
 55  banks, and parks.
 56  •
 57  Invite local skilled personnel
 58  such as gardeners, postal
 59  workers, or bankers for
 60  interaction.
 61  •
 62  Guide students in mapping
 63  routes, making posters,
 64  conducting interviews, and
 65  participating in community
 66  tasks like cleanliness
 67  drives and tree plantations.
 68  Help students to compare
 69  modes of communication
 70  in past, and present and
 71  find out how the changes
 72  have enhanced the speed
 73  and ease of doing various
 74  activities.

 10  Community Bridge Across a River
 11  In Kanker, Chhattisgarh, the Chinar river often overflows
 12  in the rainy season. Movement of goods and vehicles
 13  becomes very difficult. Reaching schools and hospitals
 14  becomes a major challenge.
 15  During one such rainy season in 2024, the entire
 16  community came together and built a simple yet strong
 17  bridge in just two days using bamboo, stones, and other
 18  material. Now, students can reach their schools and
 19  villagers can cross the river easily.
